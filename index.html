The idea

A plantain is never one thing. Green, it is starch and snap. Turning, it is bright. Ripe, it is caramel. Black, it is honey.

Most chips are made from whatever arrives at the factory that morning. Ours start with a calendar. One racimo hangs in the shade for twelve days, and we take a share of it on day 1, day 5, day 9 and day 12.

One batch at a time, the way plantain is fried on this coast.

Each day gets its own heat.

Verde goes in hot and comes out fast. Maduro goes in low, or its sugar burns before the middle is crisp.
