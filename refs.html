Pack references

The four bag fronts, drawn in code. They are the pack design, and they are also the reference image for every photo that shows a bag: the image model copies this label instead of inventing one. Saving them is free.

The button writes higgsfield/refs/<chip>-pack.png (1200 × 1600) through the local preview server (node tools/serve.mjs). On any other host, use the download link under each pack and put the files in that folder yourself.
